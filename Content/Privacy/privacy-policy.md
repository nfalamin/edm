# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** 24 August 2026

At Exclusive Download Manager (EDM), privacy and local data sovereignty are core architectural guarantees.

## Data Collection & Usage
1. **Zero URL Logging**: EDM does not log, inspect, or store URLs downloaded by clients on remote servers.
2. **Local Credential Storage**: All proxy credentials and authentication tokens are encrypted locally with DPAPI.
3. **Telemetry & Diagnostics**: Telemetry events are coarse-grained, aggregated, and anonymized (IP addresses are masked).
4. **No Third-Party Trackers**: We do not sell or monetize personal usage data.
